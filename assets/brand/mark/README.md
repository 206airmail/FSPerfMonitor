# SV monogram

A compact, angular S and V preserve the studio logo's letter pairing without its small-scale visual noise.
The lime S (#C6F33A) and cyan V (#2CD4E0) sit on a dark tile (#0B1416) with a quiet edge (#526568).
Solid filled paths, open counters and a gap between letters keep the silhouette readable at tray size.
Waiting uses identical geometry: S #D6D6D6, V #BDBDBD, tile #171717 and edge #909090, all neutral greys.
Use sv-mark-16.svg / sv-mark-16-waiting.svg at 16–20 px; their 16-unit outlines use integer coordinates.
Use sv-mark-24.svg / sv-mark-24-waiting.svg at 24 px; their 24-unit outlines have wider three-pixel stems.
Use sv-mark.svg / sv-mark-waiting.svg at 32 px and above; these are the scalable 32-unit masters.
PNG contains all six variants at 16, 20, 24, 32, 40, 48, 64, 128 and 256 px, with transparent tile corners.
preview.html and preview.png compare all sizes at 1× and 4× nearest-neighbour on both taskbar colours against the current ICOs.
Rebuild with node assets/brand/mark/render.mjs (local Chrome; no packages); tools/make_icons.py was absent from this worktree.
